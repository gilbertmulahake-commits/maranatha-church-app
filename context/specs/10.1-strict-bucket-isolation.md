# Unit 10.1: Strict Bucket Isolation & Dynamic Statuses

## Goal
Refine the financial engine and UI to treat Annual (KES 5,000) and Monthly (KES 500) contributions as completely separate ledgers with no cross-pollination of funds. Implement dynamic statuses and KPIs that accurately reflect the active `periodView` (Month vs. Year), eliminate "phantom arrears" (negative balances), and make SMS reminders context-aware.

## Design Decisions
- **Strict Bucket Isolation:** An overpayment in Annual does *not* cover a shortfall in Monthly (and vice versa). A church must fulfill both to be globally compliant.
- **Dynamic Statuses:** The status badge in tables and the counts in KPI cards must switch between `annualStatus` and `monthlyStatus` based on the toolbar toggle.
- **"N/A" Status:** If a church owes 0 in a specific bucket (e.g., joined after the March annual deadline), their status is `N/A` (neutral gray) instead of `Paid` (green).
- **Intelligent SMS:** Reminders must explicitly state what is owed (Annual vs. Monthly).

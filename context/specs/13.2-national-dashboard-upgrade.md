# Unit 13.2: National Dashboard Upgrade

## Goal
Transform the National Dashboard into a premium, high-density command center. It must feature a Hero KPI for total target progress, a live "Recent Transactions" feed, upgraded Recharts visualizations, and an improved Regional Leaderboard.

## Design
- **Hero Section:** A full-width banner showing "Total Collected" against the KES 17.6M target with a large, beautiful gradient progress bar.
- **KPI Grid:** Keep the 4 standard cards below the Hero.
- **Live Feed:** A compact list of the 5 most recent transactions to give a "live" feel.
- **Charts:** Upgrade the Bar Chart to show a smooth gradient. Add a Pie Chart for Payment Methods.
- **Leaderboard:** Upgrade the table to show a visual progress bar for each region's compliance.

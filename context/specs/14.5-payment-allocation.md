# Unit 14.5: Payment Allocation Engine (Enterprise Phase)

## Goal
Allow the system to handle mixed payments (e.g., KES 20,000 covering both Annual and Monthly dues) while preserving 1:1 bank reconciliation. Introduce a `PaymentAllocation` table to track exactly which billing periods a payment covers.

## Design Decisions
- **1:1 Bank Reconciliation:** The `Payment` table remains untouched. 1 bank deposit = 1 `Payment` record.
- **New Table:** Added `PaymentAllocation` to map a single payment to multiple years/months.
- **Year-by-Year Prepayment Priority:** When an unspecified lump sum is received, the system automatically allocates funds in this order:
  1a. Past-year Annual arrears
  1b. Past-year Monthly arrears
  2. Current-year Annual (due/overdue)
  3. Current-year Monthly arrears
  4. Current Month Monthly
  5. Remaining months of Current Year
  6+. Future Years (Annual first, then 12 months of that year, cycle by cycle)
